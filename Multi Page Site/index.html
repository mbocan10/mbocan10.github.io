Welcome

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim is an acclaimed 2011 open-world action RPG by Bethesda Game Studios. Set in the fantasy province of Skyrim, you play as the Dragonborn, destined to stop the dragon Alduin from destroying the world. It features immense freedom, a civil war storyline, and deep character customization

Main Story

In The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim, the player acts as the Dragonborn (Dovahkiin), a mortal with the power to absorb dragon souls, tasked with stopping Alduin the World-Eater, a prophecy-driven dragon returning to consume the world. Set during a brutal civil war in the snowy province of Skyrim, the hero must learn the "Way of the Voice" from the Greybeards, navigate the conflict, and ultimately defeat Alduin in the afterlife of Sovngarde.

Key Plot Points

  • The Awakening of the Dragonborn: The story begins with the player escaping the town of Helgen, destroyed by Alduin, the eldest dragon. The player then discovers they are a Dragonborn, capable of shouting and absorbing dragon souls, a power crucial to defeating Alduin.
  • The Greybeards & The Blades: The player trains with the Greybeards to master the Voice, then joins forces with The Blades (a secret dragon-hunting order).
  • The Threat & The Final Confrontation: Alduin is resurrecting other dragons to rule over Skyrim and consume the world. While the player (the Dragonborn) travels to the Dwemer ruins to find an Elder Scroll, learns the "Dragonrend" shout, and travels to the Nord afterlife, Sovngarde, to finally defeat Alduin.
  • Side Story: There is also a Civil War conflict between two factions being the Imperial Legion & the Stormcloaks. Although it is a separate, side-parallel story, through the main quest. You can also maintain a temporary peace treaty for the Civil War.
