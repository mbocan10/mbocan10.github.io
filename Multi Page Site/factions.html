Factions

Blades
In Skyrim, The Blades are a small, secretive, and nearly extinct order of elite dragon hunters, dragon lore experts, and sworn protectors of the Dragonborn. Once the personal royal guard and secret intelligence agency for the Septim Emperors, they were decimated by the Thalmor during the Great War.
College of Winterhold
The College of Winterhold is an independent magical institution in Skyrim, acting as the primary hub for arcane study, research, and training in magic. Led by Arch-Mage Savos Aren, the College is deeply mistrusted by local Nords, who blame it for the "Great Collapse" that destroyed most of Winterhold.
Companions
The Companions are a faction of warriors based in the mead hall Jorrvaskr in Whiterun, serving as the Skyrim equivalent of the Fighters Guild. They are a group of mercenaries who take on private and public contracts, upholding traditions dating back to the legendary Ysgramor. While they appear to be simple warriors, an inner circle holds a dark secret involving lycanthropy.
Dark Brotherhood
The Dark Brotherhood in The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim is an infamous guild of assassins, serving as both a, shadowy, feared, and highly secretive cult and a business that executes professional killings. By the time of the Dragon Crisis in the year (4E 201), the Brotherhood in Skyrim is a struggling, isolated remnant of its former Tamriel-wide influence, led by Astrid and operating out of a hidden sanctuary in Falkreath.
Nighingales
The Nightingales are a secretive, elite faction in Skyrim that acts as the inner circle of the Thieves Guild, dedicated to serving the Daedric Prince Nocturnal. They are guardians of the Twilight Sepulcher and act as agents of shadow, often considered a legend by lower-level guild members.
Stormcloaks
The Stormcloaks are a major, joinable rebel faction in Skyrim, led by Jarl Ulfric Stormcloak of Windhelm. They are composed primarily of Nords fighting a civil war to secede from the Third Empire, aiming to make Skyrim an independent nation, restore the open worship of Talos, and combat the influence of the Thalmor.
Imperial legion
The Imperial Legion in Skyrim is the official military force of the Third Empire, tasked with maintaining order, upholding the rule of law, and quelling the Stormcloak rebellion. Led in Skyrim by General Tullius and his second-in-command, Legate Rikke, the Legion represents a centralized government trying to keep the province united to face the future threat of the Aldmeri Dominion.
